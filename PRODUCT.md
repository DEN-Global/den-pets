# DEN Pets

## Product

DEN Pets is the real-pets and care-services marketplace for DEN members. It lets an owner keep private pet profiles and discover care services submitted by approved DEN organizations.

## First release

- Private owner-managed pet profiles.
- Search and category filters for grooming, walking, boarding, sitting, training, and day care.
- Service listings submitted by approved organizations and published after an administrator review.
- Provider listing management and administrator review queue.
- Shared DEN account sessions and same-origin APIs.

## Boundaries

This release does not include pet sales or adoption, veterinary services, booking, payment, public pet profiles, or provider contact details. Listing review is not a verification of professional qualifications. The product must not expose a pet owner's profile or exact address.

## Open product decisions

- Whether adoption and rehoming belong in a later release.
- Whether bookings and provider-to-owner contact will be supported, and how providers will be vetted.
